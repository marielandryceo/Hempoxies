# Hempoxies v.21 – All‑Hemp Vitrimer Commons

Hempoxies v.21 is an open prior‑art map and design framework for 98%+ bio‑based, dual‑dynamic vitrimer bionanocomposites built entirely from hemp‑derived feedstocks.[file:1]

## Core concept

- Epoxidized Hemp Seed Oil (EHSO) as the vitrimer matrix.[file:1]  
- Quadruple‑Function Modified Hemp Lignin (QF‑MHL, “Seshat's Lignin”) as hardener with carboxyl and tertiary amine functions.[file:1]  
- Tri‑phase hemp carbon reinforcement: nanosheets (HDCNS), carbonized hemp fibers (HDCF), and hemp biochar.[file:1]  

Together these form a conductive, self‑healing, structural composite targeting automotive chassis applications (e.g., the Landricus concept), with Joule‑heating‑based repair driven by the hemp carbon network.[file:1]

## What is open here

This repository is intended as an **open commons** around:

- Use of hemp‑derived carbons (nanosheets, fibers, biochar) in vitrimers and bio‑epoxy systems.[file:1]  
- Use of lignin (including hemp lignin) as bio‑based hardener in epoxy/vitrimer networks.[file:1]  
- Generic Joule‑heated self‑healing strategies in conductive hemp composites.[file:1]  

The `docs/Hempoxies-21.pdf` report summarises prior art in:

- Vitrimer chemistry and dynamic covalent networks.[file:1]  
- Hemp‑derived carbon materials and their synthesis.[file:1]  
- Epoxidized vegetable oils (EVOs) including EHSO.[file:1]  
- Lignin extraction and modification (organosolv, maleinization, Mannich amination).[file:1]  
- Self‑healing via Joule heating and multi‑scale reinforcement strategies.[file:1]  

## What may remain patented

Certain **narrow compositions and process windows** around QF‑MHL and tri‑phase percolation may be the subject of separate patent filings (for example, specific degrees of maleinization/amination of hemp organosolv lignin, exact wt% windows for each hemp carbon phase, and performance thresholds in structural chassis implementations).[file:1]

Contributors should assume:

- All generic patterns described in the report are donated as prior art.  
- Specific, unpublished parameter windows described only in patent filings are **not** licensed here.
